## Overview

A small Fleurieu club at Hayborough offering Nippers, patrols, surf boat and IRB racing pathways and community volunteering.

## What this club offers

- Junior / Nippers activities
- Youth participation
- Lifesaving and patrolling
- Surf sports and competition: Surf boats, Inflatable Rescue Boat racing
- Surf-sports support roles including coaching, officiating or administration

## Training, Nippers and member activity

- **Junior activities:** Saturdays from 2:00 pm.
- **Youth:** Youth program available for ages 13–18; contact the club for current session details.
- **Lifesaving and patrolling:** Typically Saturday and Sunday afternoons.
- **Surf sports:** Surf boats, Inflatable Rescue Boat racing.
- **Surf-sports support:** Coaching, officiating and administration opportunities are listed by the club.

## Membership fees and joining notes

- **New active member 18+ — $80:** Patrolling member or member training for the Surf Rescue Certificate/Bronze Medallion.
- **Social member — $80–$90:** Price varies depending on social membership type.
- **Family membership — $200:** 2 adults and children under 18.
- **Under 18 — $70–$130:** Age 5–13 plus one adult: $130; age 13–18: $70.
- **Fee note:** 2026/27 fees supplied by the club. Additional competition or program costs may apply.

## Facilities and accessibility

- Gym
- Restaurant
- Accessible toilet/changerooms

## Contact and visiting

- **Email:** [membership@chitonrocks.com](mailto:membership@chitonrocks.com)
- **Additional email:** [chitonrocks@gmail.com](mailto:chitonrocks@gmail.com)
- **When to visit:** Prospective members can generally visit from 1:00 pm on Saturdays. Contact the membership team before attending.

## Culture and club fit

- Families around Victor Harbor and Hayborough
- Members interested in surf boats or IRB racing
- People looking for a smaller volunteer-led coastal club
