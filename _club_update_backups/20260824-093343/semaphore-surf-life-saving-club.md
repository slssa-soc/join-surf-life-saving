## Overview

A large western metropolitan club offering Nippers, patrols, a Seniors pathway, broad surf sports and an adaptive program.

## What this club offers

- Junior / Nippers activities
- Youth participation
- Lifesaving and patrolling
- Surf sports and competition: Boards, Beach sprints and flags, Swimming, Ski paddling, Surf boats, Pool rescue
- Surf-sports support roles including coaching, officiating or administration
- Adaptive program available.
- No current dedicated multicultural program is listed.
- No current dedicated First Nations program is listed.

## Training, Nippers and member activity

- **Junior activities:** Saturdays 12:45 pm–3:00 pm.
- **Youth:** The club refers to its 13+ pathway as the Seniors program. Training runs from July through to the Australian Championships on multiple days of the week.
- **Lifesaving and patrolling:** Typically Saturday and Sunday afternoons.
- **Surf sports:** Boards, Beach sprints and flags, Swimming, Ski paddling, Surf boats, Pool rescue.
- **Surf-sports support:** Coaching, officiating and administration opportunities are listed by the club.

## Membership fees and joining notes

- **New active member 18+ — $220:** Patrolling member or member training for the Surf Rescue Certificate/Bronze Medallion.
- **Social/community member — $100–$200:** Price varies depending on membership type.
- **Family membership — $650:** 2 adults and up to 4 children under 18.
- **Under 18 — $155–$250:** Age 5–7: $155; age 7–13: $250; age 13–18: $200.
- **Fee note:** 2026/27 fees supplied by the club. Additional competition or program costs may apply.

## Facilities and accessibility

- Gym
- Restaurant
- Accessible toilet/changerooms
- Accessible beach access
