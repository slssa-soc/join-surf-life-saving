## About the club

Port Noarlunga SLSC is a medium-sized southern metropolitan club offering Nippers, weekend patrols and a broad range of surf sports. Members can participate across boards, beach events, swimming, ski, boats, IRB racing and pool rescue, with additional opportunities in coaching, officiating and administration. The club is well suited to families and members wanting both lifesaving and sport pathways.

## What this club offers

- Junior / Nippers activities
- Youth activities
- Lifesaving and patrolling
- Surf sports and competition: Boards, Beach sprints and flags, Swimming, Ski paddling, Surf boats, Inflatable Rescue Boat racing, Pool rescue
- Coaching, officiating and administration opportunities
- No specific adaptive program is currently listed.
- No specific multicultural program is currently listed.
- No specific First Nations program is currently listed.

## Programs and activity times

- **Junior activities:** Saturdays 9:30 am–12:00 pm.
- **Youth:** No specific youth program is currently listed.
- **Lifesaving and patrolling:** Typically Saturday and Sunday afternoons.
- **Surf sports:** Boards, Beach sprints and flags, Swimming, Ski paddling, Surf boats, Inflatable Rescue Boat racing, Pool rescue.
- **Surf-sports support:** Coaching, officiating and administration opportunities are listed by the club.

## Membership and fees

- **New active member 14+ — $150:** Patrolling member or member training for the Surf Rescue Certificate/Bronze Medallion.
- **Social member — $60:** For members who want to participate in the club without patrolling.
- **Family membership — $310:** 2 adults and all children under 18.
- **Under 14 — $220:** Age 5–13, including guardian membership.
- **Please note:** The supplied fees are 2025/26 rates. The document says the 2026/27 committee had not yet been established to review them.

## Facilities and accessibility

## Visiting the club

Visit on Saturdays or Sundays from 1:00 pm, or arrange another time by emailing the Club Secretary.

Program times, fees and facility availability can change during the season. Use the enquiry form before joining or attending for the first time.
