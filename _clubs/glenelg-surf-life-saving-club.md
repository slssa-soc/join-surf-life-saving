## About the club

Glenelg Surf Life Saving Club is a large metropolitan club with a strong community presence and a broad range of ways to get involved.

The club may suit people who are interested in lifesaving patrols, Nippers, youth development, surf sports, training and community volunteering.

## Who this club may suit

This club may suit families, new members, returning members and people looking for a club with a broad range of activities.

## Getting started

Prospective members can send their details to the club so someone can follow up with information about membership options, training and next steps.
