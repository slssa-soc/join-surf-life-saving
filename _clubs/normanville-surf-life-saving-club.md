## About the club

Normanville SLSC is a small Fleurieu club offering Nippers, weekend patrols and selected surf-sport pathways including ski paddling, surf boats and IRB racing. The club also lists a neurodivergent Nippers program and accessible facilities. It may particularly suit families seeking a smaller community club with inclusive junior participation.

## What this club offers

- Junior / Nippers activities
- Youth activities
- Lifesaving and patrolling
- Surf sports and competition: Boards, Ski paddling, Surf boats, Inflatable Rescue Boat racing
- Coaching, officiating and administration opportunities
- Neurodivergent Nippers program available.

## Programs and activity times

- **Junior activities:** Saturdays 2:00 pm–4:00 pm.
- **Youth:** Youth program available for ages 13–18; contact the club for current session details.
- **Lifesaving and patrolling:** Typically Saturday and Sunday afternoons.
- **Surf sports:** Boards, Ski paddling, Surf boats, Inflatable Rescue Boat racing.
- **Surf-sports support:** Coaching, officiating and administration opportunities are listed by the club.

## Membership and fees

- **New active member 18+ — $120:** Patrolling member or member training for the Surf Rescue Certificate/Bronze Medallion.
- **Social/community member — $50:** For members who want to participate in the club without patrolling.
- **Family membership — $200:** 2 adults and 2 children under 18; additional children $25 each.
- **Under 18 — $65:** Over 13, or under 25 while in full-time education.
- **Please note:** The supplied fee schedule was awaiting a membership vote at the 23 August 2026 AGM and should be confirmed before publication as final 2026/27 pricing.

## Facilities and accessibility

- Gym (active members only)
- Restaurant
- Accessible toilet/changerooms
- Accessible beach access

## Visiting the club

Prospective members can generally visit on Saturdays from 2:00 pm. Contact the club before attending if you would like to arrange a specific time.

Program times, fees and facility availability can change during the season. Use the enquiry form before joining or attending for the first time.
