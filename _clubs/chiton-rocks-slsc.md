## About the club

Chiton Rocks SLSC is a small Fleurieu club based at Hayborough, between Victor Harbor and Port Elliot. The club provides Nippers, youth participation, patrols and selected surf-sport pathways, including surf boats and IRB racing. Its smaller scale may appeal to members looking for a close community setting with practical opportunities to contribute.

## What this club offers

- Junior / Nippers activities
- Youth activities
- Lifesaving and patrolling
- Surf sports and competition: Surf boats, Inflatable Rescue Boat racing
- Coaching, officiating and administration opportunities

## Programs and activity times

- **Junior activities:** Saturdays from 2:00 pm.
- **Youth:** Youth program available for ages 13–18; contact the club for current session details.
- **Lifesaving and patrolling:** Typically Saturday and Sunday afternoons.
- **Surf sports:** Surf boats, Inflatable Rescue Boat racing.
- **Surf-sports support:** Coaching, officiating and administration opportunities are listed by the club.

## Membership and fees

- **New active member 18+ — $80:** Patrolling member or member training for the Surf Rescue Certificate/Bronze Medallion.
- **Social member — $80–$90:** Price varies depending on social membership type.
- **Family membership — $200:** 2 adults and children under 18.
- **Under 18 — $70–$130:** Age 5–13 plus one adult: $130; age 13–18: $70.
- **Please note:** 2026/27 fees supplied by the club. Additional competition or program costs may apply.

## Facilities and accessibility

- Gym
- Restaurant
- Accessible toilet/changerooms

## Visiting the club

Prospective members can generally visit from 1:00 pm on Saturdays. Contact the membership team before attending.

Program times, fees and facility availability can change during the season. Use the enquiry form before joining or attending for the first time.
